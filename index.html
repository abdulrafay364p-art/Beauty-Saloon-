Our Story

Where craftsmanship meets calm

Aurelle was founded on a simple belief: beauty rituals should feel as good as they look. Every chair, tool and treatment in our atelier is chosen with intention — from the herbal steam in our facials to the precision of every cut.

Our stylists and therapists train continuously with international techniques, blending modern artistry with time-honoured skincare wisdom, so every visit leaves you looking effortless and feeling restored.

  • Certified senior stylists & licensed estheticians
  • Premium, dermatologically-tested product lines
  • Private, hygienic treatment suites
